Ingest Medical Guidelines

Upload journals, clinical manuals, FDA guidelines, or safety spreadsheets. The assistant splits the text and adds semantic indices into the ChromaDB vector database.

📁

Supported Files Details

  • PDF Documents: Extracted text is segmented and assigned coordinate source descriptors (e.g. filename, page numbers).
  • Plain Text (.txt): Loaded as UTF-8 streams and chunked using sliding boundary windows.
  • CSV Spreadsheets: Parsed as row-wise interaction definitions for local rules matching.
